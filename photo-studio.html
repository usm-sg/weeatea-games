📸 Photo Studio

Turn your food photos into the pictures used inside Tea Strike, Tea Fusion and Tea Blocks.

Tap a circle to choose a photo, then drag and zoom to crop it. Photos are saved on this device only and work offline. Empty circles keep the normal emoji. Circles 1–6 also appear in Tea Match, and all 12 in Tea Mahjong.
Please use only photos you took yourself or have permission to use.

A pack file (teapack.json) holds all your photos in one small file. Save it as a backup, send it to yourself, or load it on another device. Putting it next to the game files makes the games use those photos for everyone.
